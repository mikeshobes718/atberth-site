Firebase alternative

A Firebase alternative built on Postgres, with a price you can predict

Berth covers what most apps use Firebase for, sign in, a database, file storage, realtime updates, functions and hosting, on a relational Postgres database, for a flat monthly price.

What is the same

  • Everything for an app in one place. Auth with Apple and Google, storage, realtime updates, server functions and hosting for your site.
  • Client keys that are safe to ship. Phones and browsers use a publishable key and a user token, and every table has a policy.

What is different

  • SQL instead of documents. Firestore is a NoSQL document store; Postgres on Firebase goes through Data Connect. On Berth every app is a Postgres database you can query, join and export with standard tools.
  • No surprise bills. Firebase's Blaze plan bills as you go, and its budget alerts do not pause services. Berth's limits are hard caps: at a limit it refuses the request instead of charging more.
  • A flat price. Berth Pro is $12 a month for the first 100 accounts.

Moving over

Export your collections as JSON or CSV and load them with berth import. Your users can keep signing in with Apple or Google. Or ask us to move it for you.
