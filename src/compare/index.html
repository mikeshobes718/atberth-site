Where Berth fits

  • One service instead of two. A typical setup pairs front-end hosting with a separate backend. On Berth the database, sign in, storage, functions and site share one account, one CLI and one set of keys.
  • A bill that cannot surprise you. Every limit is a hard cap. At a limit Berth refuses the request; it never adds usage charges.
  • Free apps stay on. Free apps are never paused for being idle.
  • We can build it for you. Need the site or app as well as the hosting? We design, build and launch it on Berth. Tell us what you need, and moving from Supabase has its own guide.

Where the others fit better

  • Global scale. Vercel serves from a worldwide edge network, and Supabase and Firebase let you pick regions. Berth runs in the United States.
  • Guarantees. Berth offers an uptime SLA only in Scale contracts, and publishes its measured uptime. Larger providers sell enterprise plans with broader SLAs and compliance programs.
  • Ecosystem. Vercel, Supabase and Firebase have years of integrations, templates and community answers behind them.

If your app needs those today, they are the better choice. If you want one place for a new app's whole backend and a price you can predict, start free on Berth.
