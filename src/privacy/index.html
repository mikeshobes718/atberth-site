Privacy

Privacy

What Berth collects when you use atberth.com, the web console, the berth CLI and the Berth Console app for iPhone, and why. Last updated September 27, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, when the account was created and verified, and when you last signed in. We use it to sign you in with emailed codes and to contact you about the service.
  • Sign-in codes and keys. One-time sign-in codes and account keys. Keys are stored only as hashes, so we cannot read them back.
  • Your apps' data. Everything your apps store on Berth: database rows, files, functions, env vars, webhooks, sites and your apps' end users. We store it so your apps can use it and do not use it for anything else.
  • Billing. If you pay for Pro, Stripe handles the payment. We keep your plan and Stripe's customer and subscription IDs, never your card details.
  • Service logs. Request logs, including IP addresses, to run the service, show you your apps' usage, fix problems and stop abuse.

The iPhone app

Berth Console for iPhone talks only to the Berth API. Your sign-in key is kept in the phone's keychain. The app has no analytics, ads or tracking, and collects nothing beyond what the Berth service itself stores for your account.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. Fonts are served from this site. If you pick a light or dark theme, that choice is saved in your own browser only.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers in the United States. Each app's database is backed up every night, and those backups are kept so data can be restored after a problem.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers that run the service (server hosting, email delivery for sign-in codes, and Stripe for payments), or when the law requires it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and every app in it at any time: in the iPhone app under Account, then Delete account; with berth account delete; or by emailing us. Deletion removes your apps, their databases and files, and cancels any plan. Copies in nightly backups expire as the backups roll over.

Apps hosted on Berth

Apps that run on Berth have their own privacy policies. See hosted apps.
