Clip Support

Clip records a voice memo or takes a video or audio file you pick, then uploads it to your Media inbox so you can transcribe or describe it.

Quick start

  1. Open Clip. The inbox URL and key can be set in Settings. A default inbox is baked into the build.
  2. Tap Record a memo, or pick a video from Photos, or pick a file. You can also share from Voice Memos, Photos, or Files into Clip.
  3. Wait until it says Uploaded. Clip copies a line you can paste, such as: Transcribe the latest clip from my phone.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no Clip login.

Clip is missing from the share sheet. Open the sheet, tap Edit, and enable Clip.

Upload failed. Check the network, then tap retry on the failed row. Offline clips go out when service returns.

Microphone or Photos is grey. Allow access in iOS Settings, Privacy, then open Clip again.
