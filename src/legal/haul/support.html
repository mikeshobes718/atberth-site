Haul Support

Haul sorts shopping dumps, tracks a weekly grocery budget, and keeps pantry stock and meal plans on your iPhone.

Quick start

  1. Open Today and type or speak a messy list (any language). Tap to sort. Confirm the items.
  2. Use Lists for groceries by store, online buys, and errands. Mark things done as you go.
  3. Scan a receipt on Today to fill prices into the weekly budget.
  4. Stock holds pantry items, supplier orders, and meal plans. Places holds each city or home with its own currency and budget.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Lists stay on this phone.

Sorting failed or said unreadable. Check the network, then try again. For a product photo, use Retry with Astra on the result screen.

Camera is grey. Force restart the iPhone (volume up, volume down, hold the side button), then open Haul again. This is an iOS camera issue after some camera sessions, not a missing permission.

Location. Allow When In Use so Haul can pick the current place. Always is only for store arrival nudges, and you can leave that off.
